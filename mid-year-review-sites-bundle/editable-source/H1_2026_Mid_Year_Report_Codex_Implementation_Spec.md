### Section introduction

**Business Building & Incubation**

Building upon past incubation success, Prologis Ventures is creating an incubation engine that supports the creation of new businesses that strengthen the Prologis platform and generate new revenue. The investment in Fulfillment IQ established CrossDock Studios, our incubation studio that combines Prologis’ deep understanding of customer challenges with Fulfillment IQ’s product development capabilities to rapidly build and commercialize new businesses.

Every initiative follows a structured path through evaluating customer pain points, discovery, rapid prototyping, design partner validation, and iteration. Depending on the opportunity, products may become standalone venture-backed companies, future Prologis Ventures investments, Operating Essentials offerings, or consulting solutions that support both companies.

### Workbench

Warehouse design remains a manual process that requires months of engineering analysis across facility layouts, labor planning, automation design, and financial modeling before a customer can make an investment decision. Workbench will dramatically accelerate that process by bringing warehouse design, simulation, and AI into a single platform. The initial release of Workbench is designed to help 3PL customers convert their customers’ warehouse requirements into optimized facility designs in hours instead of months. NFI has signed as the first paying design partner with a $200K annual commitment, while GXO, DHL, GEODIS, and Yusen are in discussions. Phase One is scheduled for delivery in October 2026.

The larger opportunity for Workbench is to become a digital twin and simulation engine for warehouse operations. Developed alongside NVIDIA on the Omniverse platform, Prologis will be able to simulate automation strategies, warehouse layouts, labor models, throughput, and network decisions before making physical investments. As more customer and operational data is incorporated into the platform, Workbench will become the operating system for warehouse operations. Prologis can help a customer model their entire network, compare investment scenarios, and simulate operational performance before spending a dollar on infrastructure or automation. This positions Prologis as a trusted partner in helping customers make critical decisions, creating a significant new software and advisory revenue opportunity while increasing pull-through across Operating Essentials and more.

### WareSight

Every customer facility assessment and Operating Essentials product recommendation depends on the experience of the ESM conducting the walkthrough. WareSight transforms that experience into an AI-powered operational assessment. As an ESM walks a facility with a customer, the platform records the conversation, creates a voice-enabled assessment in real time, analyzes operational observations, and recommends solutions across Operating Essentials offerings. WareSight can operate even in low-connectivity environments, allowing recommendations to be generated during the customer visit. Live customer pilots are expected to begin this fall.

### Zynque

Zynque demonstrates how CrossDock Studios is able to incubate and create venture-backable standalone businesses. Zynque is a logistics-native integration and orchestration platform designed to connect warehouse management systems, transportation systems, robotics, ERP platforms, and other warehouse technologies through a common integration layer built specifically for logistics environments. Prologis Ventures is working with Fulfillment IQ to spin Zynque out of the business, taking the company to market to raise a first tranche of venture capital from both institutional and strategic investors.
