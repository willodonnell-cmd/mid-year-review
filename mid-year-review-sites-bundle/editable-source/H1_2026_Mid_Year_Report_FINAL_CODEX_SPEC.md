# 3. Key Portfolio Updates

## Market Context and Strategic Positioning

Venture capital conditions are improving. In 2025, U.S. venture investment reached $340B, the second-strongest year on record. The trend continued into 2026, with global startup funding reaching $510B in the first half of the year, driven largely by AI; OpenAI and Anthropic alone accounted for 43% of all startup funding. Although overall deal activity remains below peak levels, investors are showing renewed confidence in companies that demonstrate durable growth, capital efficiency, and a clear path to profitability.

The liquidity window is also reopening after several years of limited exits. Venture-backed IPO activity accelerated through the end of 2025 and into 2026, including public offerings from Figma, CoreWeave, Chime, and BETA Technologies, while strategic M&A returned at scale. In March, Google completed its $32B acquisition of Wiz, the largest acquisition of a venture-backed cybersecurity company and Google’s largest acquisition to date.

This improving backdrop is increasingly reflected in the Prologis Ventures portfolio, where several strategic companies have active exit or liquidity paths underway. While outcomes remain company specific, the market is more favorable than at any point since 2021, creating increased opportunities to generate returns over the next six to twelve months.

# 3. Business Unit Acceleration

## Essentials

Following Prologis’ January investment in Fulfillment IQ, Prologis formalized a strategic partnership that combines Fulfillment IQ’s supply chain consulting, technology development, and automation expertise with Essentials’ scale and infrastructure capabilities. The partnership expands Prologis’ ability to engage customers earlier in their supply chain transformation by offering strategy, implementation, software development, and infrastructure through a single customer-engagement model.

During the first half of the year, Prologis Ventures introduced Essentials to Albertsons, leading to discussions with CLD around a four-site cold-chain build-to-suit opportunity, automation, yard operations, energy resilience, and network optimization. Albertsons expects to invest approximately $4B in material handling and automation across 22 distribution centers over the next five years, creating opportunities spanning real estate, Essentials, Energy, and future technology solutions.

Ventures is also expanding customer access to early warehouse automation. Ventures introduced customers to autonomous forklift technology through Gideon, with Essentials establishing a referral relationship with Gideon. Unit AI has a growing pipeline of Prologis customers seeking to participate in early deployments, demonstrating customer demand for returns-logistics solutions. The investment in Walden Robotics will enable a customer cohort to help shape the first logistics use cases of human-agentic technology based on shared warehouse pain points.

## Development

Anori, an AI platform that streamlines development and entitlement workflows, completed its nine-month spinout from Google X at the start of the year. Ventures has since led working sessions with Prologis Development, Capital Deployment, Government Affairs, and Data Centers to identify the highest-value challenges for Anori to address. The team has helped shape the platform around entitlement analysis, zoning and permitting workflows, site feasibility, regulatory review, community sentiment, and development planning.

Anori has developed its first product, and structured validation is underway on 2–3 Prologis projects to measure speed, accuracy, completeness, actionability, and workflow fit before determining the first repeatable production workflow. The long-term objective is to improve entitlement preparedness, reduce manual research, strengthen underwriting assumptions, and improve how development teams evaluate and prioritize projects across the portfolio.

Ventures is also working with Development and Prologis Labs/IT to identify where AI can create durable advantages across the development lifecycle. After evaluating more than 100 AI-native real estate companies, the team concluded that the largest opportunity is not automating individual tasks, but building AI-enabled workflows that improve capital allocation, compress project timelines, strengthen underwriting, reduce entitlement and cost risk, and continuously improve decision-making. Current work includes a proof of concept with Tangible as part of the Cost IQ initiative. The pilot will test whether AI can reduce by 50–90% the up to 40 hours development teams currently spend estimating construction material quantities, enabling faster project evaluation and more accurate early-stage cost forecasting.

## Data Centers

Prologis’ relationship with Ventures portfolio company XNRGY has progressed into a strategic operating partnership. Ventures worked with the Data Center team to execute a signed LOI that positions Prologis within XNRGY’s production planning for approximately 2 GW of potential projects where XNRGY’s technology could be deployed. The partnership creates access to mission-critical cooling infrastructure at a time when manufacturing capacity has become one of the largest constraints facing hyperscale development.

In parallel, Ventures continues to evaluate AI infrastructure and cooling technologies with the Data Center team, including SF Compute, Together AI, and Electio, to better understand the data center transaction landscape and strengthen the long-term execution strategy of the Data Center platform. This work focuses on computing infrastructure, advanced cooling, and the technologies that connect power, thermal management, and capacity planning. The objective is to identify where Prologis can secure differentiated access, reduce execution constraints, and strengthen its position as data center demand grows.

## Energy & Sustainability

Prologis OnPrem Power delivers dispatchable on-site power that enables customers to bring facilities online faster. Over the last year, Ventures helped transition OnPrem from an incubation into an operating business by supporting its commercial strategy, governance model, customer offering, and transaction framework. Ventures also supported the Energy team’s first major commercial opportunity this year with Quanta, an engineering and infrastructure customer evaluating OnPrem as part of a large-scale power deployment. That work validated the commercial model and established underwriting processes.

Ventures continues to support OnPrem as it expands its commercial pipeline, including Walmart, Amazon, and Prologis’ Data Centers platform.

With the Prologis Sustainability team, Ventures invested in Paebbl to expand access to low-carbon concrete technologies for future European developments. Paebbl recently conducted a pour with Amazon that achieved better performance standards than the current preferred concrete. TerraCO2 is progressing its manufacturing facility in Cleburne, TX and is exploring opportunities with the construction team to test at nearby warehouses once complete. Ventures also expanded Prologis’ partnership with SOLARCYCLE by executing an exclusivity agreement for end-of-life solar panel recycling, strengthening both Prologis’ and customers’ circularity strategy.

## Industry Research

These customer discussions also informed broader industry research. Most recently, Ventures worked with Prologis Research and Silicon Valley Bank to produce the [SVB x Prologis Ventures Physical AI and the Future of Robotics Report](http://svb.com/trends-insights/reports/physical-ai-robotics/?_gl=1*s00u64*_up*MQ..gs*MQ..&gclid=CjwKCAjw1IHTBhAaEiwA4AYNFhxulRoNwoMj0xHY-sBlGgYj_8F5x5Hp398MxrLH4yK0u7yZixuBeRoCpmoQAvD_BwE&gbraid=0AAAAADGihGFQzoiNW0CVdj9NJAx*--l0B), extending many of the themes heard throughout the first half of the year to a broader survey of PrologisNext executives. **Warehouse operators are investing in AI where it saves time today.**  
The most common use cases were reporting and analytics, document automation, and labor planning, while more advanced applications remain in the evaluation stage.

**Customers are becoming more confident in AI and automation, but they expect measurable results.**

## Introduction

Building on past incubation success, Prologis Ventures is creating an incubation engine that supports the creation of new businesses that strengthen the Prologis platform and generate new revenue. The investment in Fulfillment IQ established CrossDock Studios, an incubation studio that combines Prologis’ understanding of customer challenges with Fulfillment IQ’s product-development capabilities to rapidly build and commercialize new businesses.

Each initiative follows a structured path through customer-pain-point evaluation, discovery, rapid prototyping, design-partner validation, and iteration. Depending on the opportunity, products may become standalone venture-backed companies, future Prologis Ventures investments, Essentials offerings, or consulting solutions that support both companies.

## Workbench

Warehouse design remains a manual process that requires months of engineering analysis across facility layouts, labor planning, automation design, and financial modeling before a customer can make an investment decision. Workbench is designed to accelerate that process by bringing warehouse design, simulation, and AI into a single platform. Its initial release will help 3PL customers convert warehouse requirements into optimized facility designs in hours instead of months. NFI has signed as the first paying design partner with a $200K annual commitment, while GXO, DHL, GEODIS, and Yusen are finalizing contracting. Phase One is scheduled for delivery in October 2026.

NVIDIA is a partner in the development of Workbench, which has been presented to Jensen Huang and NVIDIA management. Developed on NVIDIA Omniverse, Workbench is intended to become a digital twin and simulation engine for warehouse operations. It will enable Prologis to simulate automation strategies, warehouse layouts, labor models, throughput, and network decisions before making physical investments. As customer and operational data is incorporated into the platform, Workbench will help customers model networks, compare investment scenarios, and simulate operational performance before investing in infrastructure or automation. This positions Prologis to create the operating system for supply chain and logistics—combining warehouse design, simulation, operational intelligence, and decision support across the customer network.

## WareSight

Every customer facility assessment and Essentials product recommendation depends on the experience of the ESM conducting the walkthrough. WareSight transforms that experience into an AI-powered operational assessment. As an ESM walks a facility with a customer, the platform records the conversation, creates a voice-enabled assessment in real time, analyzes operational observations, and recommends solutions across Essentials offerings.

WareSight can operate in low-connectivity environments, allowing recommendations to be generated during the customer visit. Live customer pilots are expected to begin this fall.

## Zynque

Zynque demonstrates CrossDock Studios’ ability to incubate and create venture-backable standalone businesses. It is a logistics-native integration and orchestration platform designed to connect warehouse management systems, transportation systems, robotics, ERP platforms, and other warehouse technologies through a common integration layer built specifically for logistics environments.

Prologis Ventures is working with Fulfillment IQ to spin Zynque out of the business and take the company to market for an initial venture-capital raise from institutional and strategic investors.
