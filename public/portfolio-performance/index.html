How this reporting suite works

Direct equity: PLDV transaction rows are the source of truth. LP investments: reconciled dated capital calls drive invested capital, unfunded commitments, and schedule XIRR; position values remain the current-value source. MOIC: current value ÷ invested capital. XIRR: actual investment and realization dates on a 365-day basis through July 19, 2026. Uncalled commitments, cost-of-capital, and overhead are excluded from portfolio return calculations.

Model documentation

Methodology and calculation notes

Documentation of the data sources, calculation methods, assumptions, and identified exceptions supporting this report.

Model basisReconciled July 19, 2026
1

Source hierarchy

PLDV is authoritative for direct-equity transaction dates, principal, current value, stage, security, and status. LP position headers are authoritative for commitments and current value; dated capital-call rows are authoritative for calculated called capital, unfunded capital, and XIRR. Reporting is retained as a comparator rather than a replacement for the underlying schedules.

2

Company chronology

Companies are ordered by their earliest investment date. Transactions inside each company block are ordered by investment date, then by original PLDV row sequence for same-day activity. Every company ends with a principal, value, MOIC, and XIRR subtotal.

3

MOIC calculation

Transaction MOIC equals current value divided by principal. Company, vintage, fund, and consolidated MOIC equal the sum of current values divided by the sum of principal or called capital. Rows with a displayed zero investment basis are excluded from independent MOIC reproduction and flagged.

4

XIRR calculation

Each investment or capital call is a negative cash flow on its actual date. Current or realized value is a positive cash flow on the workbook as-of or realization date. The solver uses Excel-style actual-day timing over 365 days. Uncalled commitments do not enter XIRR.

5

Fund consolidation

Fund invested capital combines direct-equity principal with dated LP capital calls. Fund current value combines direct-equity value with LP position value. Schedule XIRR uses dated direct transactions and dated LP calls; LP header balances remain visible as reconciliation comparators.

6

Cash-flow rebuild

Investment outflows are assigned to their actual transaction or call year. Non-active direct values are treated as realized on their row as-of date. Active direct values and LP position values are treated as unrealized value at July 19, 2026. Workbook cost-of-capital and overhead allocations are excluded because they are not portfolio investment cash flows.
